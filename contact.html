Frequently Asked Questions

How is the AI Trust Score computed?
Our Trust Engine evaluates uploaded freehold title deeds, municipal encumbrance filings, seller historical reputation, and price-to-market sanity deviations to compute a transparent 0–100 rating.
What is Property DNA?
Property DNA is a structured digital fingerprint recording the exact structural age, engineering certifications, historical ownership chains, and architectural warranty milestones for complete transparency.
Are hidden closing costs accurate?
Yes, our engine applies jurisdictional statutory tax rates, stamp duty formulas, average HOA reserves, and mandatory registration fees to give you a true first-year budget expectation.
